The results these notes describe are published as a browsable catalogue, the **Spectrum Atlas**:
which molecules the pipeline lists, computes, anchors and validates, with the status ladder, the harmonic
frequencies, the structures, and the provenance of every number (each one links to the file it came from).

**Open the atlas: <https://thebreadishard.github.io/spectrum-atlas/>**

Good places to start: [naphthalene](https://thebreadishard.github.io/spectrum-atlas/molecule/A_01f3186607/), the molecule
with a coupled-cluster anchor; the [Methods](https://thebreadishard.github.io/spectrum-atlas/methods/) page with the
architecture sheets and the three lessons; and [Status](https://thebreadishard.github.io/spectrum-atlas/status/) for the
counts, the releases and the project's changelog. Reading needs no account; the site has no trackers.
